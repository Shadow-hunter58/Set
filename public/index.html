How to Play SET!

🎯 1. The Goal

Collect 4 cards of the same item (4x Strawberries, 4x Teslas, 4x Lions). The first player to collect 4-of-a-kind and slap the center pile wins!

🔄 2. Passing Cards

Each player starts with 4 random cards. On your turn, pick 1 card to pass clockwise to the next player. Keep cards that match!

📱 3. Phone Multiplayer

Host a room on one device, show the QR code. All your friends scan the QR with their phones to join the same table!

⚡ 4. Winning SET!

As soon as you hold 4 matching cards, SLAP the glowing center pile to claim your victory!
